Futile Surgery Risk Score

A prognostic risk score for futile surgery (recurrence or death within six months of resection) in gallbladder cancer, derived from the OMEGA global multicentre cohort. It stratifies the risk of futile surgery and can inform preoperative decision-making alongside adequate staging.

How the score works

Four readily available clinicopathological variables were independently associated with futile surgery on multivariable logistic regression and combined into a points-based score. The total score maps to a predicted probability of futile surgery:

Total scorePredicted probability of futile surgery

Model discrimination: area under the curve 0.760, in a cohort of 2573 patients of whom 504 (19.6%) had futile surgery. Performance was maintained across high and low or middle income settings and across high and low prevalence regions (area under the curve 0.75–0.77), and the score showed net clinical benefit on decision curve analysis across threshold probabilities of approximately 6% to 56%.

Intended use and limitations. This calculator is a research decision-support tool intended to inform, not replace, multidisciplinary clinical judgement. It is based on internal validation within the OMEGA cohort and awaits external and prospective validation. It was developed in patients undergoing up-front surgery without neoadjuvant chemotherapy and has not been validated in the post-neoadjuvant setting. No patient-identifiable data are entered, stored, or transmitted; all calculations run locally in your browser.
